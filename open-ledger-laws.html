The architecture

The Four Conservation Laws.

Why the ledger stays whole. These are the laws the record obeys — stated as principle, not checked as audit. Nothing is pooled, so there is nothing to reconcile. The record is the proof.

Mass
What enters is divided exactly 80 / 16 / 3 / 1, to the colón. Nothing pools, nothing leaks at the split.
Energy
Value flows through unbroken — recorded as it routes to custody, never held or pooled.
Information
Every event is recorded — complete, under two kilobytes, no transcript stored.
Generational
Value is preserved across time: transact local, reserve in CHF.

Conserved at every node — the loop is closed because nothing leaks.

BRASA never handles, holds, or settles money. The rail is SINPE, operated by the BCCR; BRASA records the payment and issues the Hacienda tiquete (v4.4). No simulation.
