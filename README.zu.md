# I-BRASA World

**Uhlelo olulodwa oluxhunyiwe lwemfundo, uhulumeni, ibhizinisi, kanye nokusobala komphakathi—olwakhelwe wonke umuntu.**

[Vakashela i-BRASA World](https://brasa.world/) · [Imfundo](https://brasa.education/) · [Uhulumeni](https://brasagovernment.net/) · [Ibhizinisi](https://brasa.business/)

I-BRASA ixhumanisa ulwazi lomphakathi, izinsizakalo zezakhamuzi, imfundo, kanye nezentengiselwano ngaphandle kokushintsha umthombo wesiNgisi onika amandla uhlelo lomhlaba wonke. Amakhasi ezwe aqondisa isivakashi ngasinye ezikhungweni, ezikoleni, izinsizakalo, kanye namabhizinisi afanele lapho sihlala khona.

## Izinsika ezine

- **Imfundo:** thola izikole, amanyuvesi, izifundo, kanye nokufunda impilo yonke.
- **Uhulumeni:** zulazula ongqongqoshe, izikhungo zomphakathi, izinsiza, kanye nezinsizakalo zezakhamuzi.
- **Ibhizinisi:** amathuluzi wamahhala ezakhamuzi ukuqala, ukusebenza, nokukhulisa ibhizinisi.
- **I-BRASA:** umnyango womphakathi oqashelwa yizwe oxhumanisa uhlelo oluphelele.

## Izwe elakhiwe ngamazwe

I-BRASA igcina ikhasi lezwe lazo zonke izizwe ezisekelwayo. Ikhasi ngalinye lisebenzisa umklamo owabiwe womhlaba wonke ngenkathi lixhumanisa nezikhungo zikahulumeni wangempela walelo zwe, izinsiza zemfundo, kanye nezinsizakalo.

## Ukufinyelela kolimi

IsiNgisi ulimi lomthombo olugcinwe yi-BRASA. Izivakashi zingasebenzisa inguqulo yendawo ngenkathi abahleli beqhubeka nokusebenza kusukela kuzinga elifanayo lesiNgisi. Izinguqulo ezishicilelwe zithola i-URL yazo, imethadatha yolimi, isimo sokubuyekeza, kanye nesixhumanisi esibuyela emuva esiNgisini.

## Qala lapha

Vakashela i-[brasa.world](https://brasa.world/) bese ukhetha izwe lakho, ulimi, noma insika.

## Nikela

Siza ukuqinisekisa izwe, ubuyekeze ukuhumusha, ubike isixhumanisi somphakathi esiphelelwe yisikhathi, noma uthuthukise ukufinyeleleka. Ukunemba nolwazi lwendawo oluwusizo luza ngaphambi kwevolumu yokuhumusha.

## BRASA namuhla

BRASA manje ixhumanisa izindawo zamazwe angu-198 neMfundo, uHulumeni kanye neBhizinisi. BRASA Business inezindawo eziyi-12 ezixhunyiwe: ukuthengisa namarisidi, CRM, impahla, abantu nokuqeqeshwa, amaholo namabhange, isikhathi, ukwesekwa komndeni, umsebenzi wezomthetho, amafomu kahulumeni, imibiko nendawo yokusebenza ehlanganyelwe. Ihlanganisa nebhuku lezimali elivulekile, izindlela zokusebenza eziqondisiwe nokuqhubeka ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi.

BRASA Financials ibala izinhlelo zokukhokha ezingama-385 ezivela kumarekhodi angama-395, ngezincazelo zemakethe eziyi-161 nezinhlobo zokukhokha ezingama-216: imali yeselula, ukudlulisa ngokushesha, QR, izikhwama zedijithali, amanethiwekhi emali, ukuthumela imali, open banking, amakhadi asekhaya kanye ne-USSD.

Ukufakwa ohlwini akusho ukuthi sekuvuliwe. Abahlinzeki abanelayisensi bahambisa imali; indlela ngayinye idinga ukubuyekezwa kwezwe nomthombo, ilayisensi, ukuhlanganiswa, ukuhlolwa kokukhokhelana kanye nemvume yokusebenza.
