# BRASA World

** Sisitemu imwe ihuza uburezi, guverinoma, ubucuruzi, no gukorera mu mucyo-byubatswe kuri buri muturage. **

[Sura BRASA World](https://brasa.world/) · [Uburezi](https://brasa.education/) · [Guverinoma](https://brasagovernment.net/) · [Ubucuruzi](https://brasa.business/)

BRASA ihuza ubumenyi rusange, serivisi zabaturage, uburezi, nubucuruzi idahinduye inkomoko yicyongereza iha imbaraga isi yose. Urupapuro rwigihugu ruyobora buri mushyitsi mubigo, amashuri, serivisi, nubucuruzi bijyanye n’aho batuye.

## Inkingi enye

- ** Uburezi: ** shakisha amashuri, kaminuza, amasomo, no kwiga ubuzima bwawe bwose.
- ** Guverinoma: ** kuyobora minisiteri, ibigo bya leta, ibikorwa rusange, na serivisi zabaturage.
- ** Ubucuruzi: ** ibikoresho byubusa kubaturage gutangira, gukora, no guteza imbere ubucuruzi.
- ** BRASA: ** umuryango-uzi neza ubwinjiriro rusange buhuza sisitemu yuzuye.

## Yubatswe igihugu nigihugu

BRASA ikora page yigihugu kuri buri gihugu gishyigikiwe. Buri paji ikoresha igishushanyo mbonera gisangiwe kwisi yose mugihe ihuza ibigo bya leta nyabyo, umutungo wuburezi, na serivisi.

## Kubona ururimi

Icyongereza ni BRASA imvugo ikomeza. Abashyitsi barashobora gukoresha verisiyo yaho mugihe abanditsi bakomeje gukora uhereye kumurongo umwe wicyongereza. Ubuhinduzi bwasohotse bwakira URL zabo, metadata yururimi, gusubiramo imiterere, no guhuza icyongereza.

## Tangira hano

Sura [brasa.world](https://brasa.world/) hanyuma uhitemo igihugu cyawe, ururimi, cyangwa inkingi.

## Tanga umusanzu

Fasha kugenzura igihugu, gusubiramo ibisobanuro, gutanga raporo ihuza abantu bataye igihe, cyangwa kunoza uburyo bworoshye. Ibisobanuro namakuru yingirakamaro byaho biza mbere yubunini bwubusobanuro.

## BRASA uyu munsi

BRASA ubu ihuza aho ibihugu 198 bigana n'Uburezi, Guverinoma n'Ubucuruzi. BRASA Business ifite ibice 12 bihujwe: kugurisha n'inyemezabwishyu, CRM, ibarura, abakozi n'amahugurwa, imishahara na banki, igihe, ubufasha bw'umuryango, amategeko, impapuro za leta, raporo n'ahantu ho gukorera hamwe. Harimo kandi igitabo cy'ibaruramari gifunguye, inzira z'akazi ziyobowe no gukomeza gukora nta murandasi.

BRASA Financials ikataloge sisitemu 385 zo kwishyura zivuye mu nyandiko 395, zirimo ibisobanuro by'amasoko 161 n'ubwoko 216 bwo kwishyura: amafaranga ya telefoni, kohereza ako kanya, QR, amakofi y'ikoranabuhanga, imiyoboro y'amafaranga, kohereza amafaranga, open banking, amakarita yo mu gihugu na USSD.

Kuba muri kataloge ntibisobanura ko byatangiye gukora. Abatanga serivisi bafite uruhushya ni bo bimura amafaranga; buri buryo bugomba kugenzurwa ku gihugu n'inkomoko, uruhushya, ihuzwa, igerageza ry'ubwishyu n'icyemezo cyo gukoresha.
