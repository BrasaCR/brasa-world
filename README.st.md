# BRASA World

**Sistimi e le 'ngoe e hokahantsoeng ea thuto, mmuso, khoebo, le ponaletso ea sechaba—e etselitsoeng moahi e mong le e mong.**

[Etela BRASA World](https://brasa.world/) · [Thuto](https://brasa.education/) · [Mmuso](https://brasagovernment.net/) · [Khoebo](https://brasa.business/)

BRASA e hokahanya tsebo ea sechaba, litšebeletso tsa baahi, thuto, le khoebo ntle le ho fetola mohloli oa Senyesemane o laolang tsamaiso ea lefats'e. Maqephe a naha a tataisa moeti e mong le e mong ho ea ho litsi, likolo, litšebeletso, le likhoebo tse amanang le moo a lulang teng.

## Litšiea tse 'nè

- **Thuto:** fumana likolo, liunivesithi, lithuto, le thuto ea bophelo bohle.
- **Mmuso:** tsamaisa makala, litsi tsa sechaba, lits'ebeletso, le litšebeletso tsa baahi.
- **Khoebo:** lisebelisoa tsa mahala bakeng sa baahi ho qala, ho sebetsa le ho holisa khoebo.
- **BRASA:** keno ea sechaba e tsebang naha e hokahanyang sistimi e felletseng.

## Naha e ahiloeng ka naha

BRASA e boloka leqephe la naha bakeng sa sechaba se seng le se seng se tšehelitsoeng. Leqephe le leng le le leng le sebelisa moralo o arolelanoang lefatšeng ka bophara ha le ntse le hokahana le litsi tsa 'muso tsa naha eo, lisebelisoa tsa thuto le lits'ebeletso.

## Ho fihlella puo

Senyesemane ke puo ea mohloli e hlokometsoeng ea BRASA. Baeti ba ka sebelisa mofuta oa lehae ha bahlophisi ba ntse ba tsoela pele ho sebetsa ho latela maemo a tšoanang a Senyesemane. Liphetolelo tse hatisitsoeng li fumana URL ea tsona, boitsebiso ba puo, boemo ba maikutlo, le lihokelo tsa ho khutlela ho Senyesemane.

## Qala mona

Etela [brasa.world](https://brasa.world/) ebe u khetha naha ea heno, puo, kapa tšiea.

## Kenya letsoho

Thusa ho netefatsa naha, ho hlahloba phetolelo, tlaleha sehokelo sa sechaba se siiloeng ke nako, kapa ho ntlafatsa mokhoa oa ho sebelisana le bona. Ho nepahala le boitsebiso bo molemo ba sebaka ka seng bo tla pele ho bolumo ea phetolelo.

## BRASA kajeno

BRASA jwale e hokahanya dibaka tsa dinaha tse 198 le Thuto, Mmuso le Kgwebo. BRASA Business e na le dikarolo tse 12 tse hokahaneng: thekiso le dirasiti, CRM, setoko, batho le thupelo, meputso le dibanka, nako, tshehetso ya lelapa, molao, diforomo tsa mmuso, ditlaleho le sebaka se kopanetsweng sa mosebetsi. E boetse e kenyelletsa lejara e bulehileng, ditsamaiso tse tataiswang le tshebetso ntle le inthanete.

BRASA Financials e thathamisa ditsamaiso tsa tefo tse 385 ho tswa direkotong tse 395, ka ditlhaloso tsa mebaraka tse 161 le mefuta ya tefo e 216: tjhelete ya mohala, phetisetso ya hanghang, QR, dipotlana tsa dijithale, marangrang a tjhelete, remitense, open banking, dikarete tsa lehae le USSD.

Ho ba lethathamong ha ho bolele hore ho se ho butswe. Bafani ba nang le laesense ke bona ba tsamaisang tjhelete; mokgwa o mong le o mong o hloka tlhahlobo ya naha le mohlodi, laesense, kopanyo, teko ya tefo le tumello ya tlhahiso.
