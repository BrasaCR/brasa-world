How you earn a degree or certificate

How earning works

A degree with its proof

When you finish a path — say, an MBA — the degree is issued as a signed credential that bundles every course that earned it. Scan your QR and you see the degree and the whole trail behind it, not just the title.

Verifiable in seconds

The record carries its own signature chain back to BRASA's public key. An employer scans once — no phone calls, no paid services — and the proof either checks out or it doesn't.
