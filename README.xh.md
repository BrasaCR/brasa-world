# BRASA World

**Inkqubo enye eqhagamshelweyo yemfundo, urhulumente, amashishini, kunye nokwenziwa elubala koluntu—eyakhelwe wonke ummi.**

[Ndwendwela iBRASA World](https://brasa.world/) · [Imfundo](https://brasa.education/) · [Urhulumente](https://brasagovernment.net/) · [Ishishini](https://brasa.business/)

I-BRASA idibanisa ulwazi loluntu, iinkonzo zabemi, imfundo, kunye norhwebo ngaphandle kokutshintsha umthombo wesiNgesi onika amandla inkqubo yehlabathi. Amaphepha elizwe akhokela umtyeleli ngamnye kumaziko, izikolo, iinkonzo, kunye namashishini afanelekileyo apho ahlala khona.

## Iintsika ezine

- **Imfundo:** ukufumana izikolo, iiyunivesithi, iikhosi, kunye nokufunda ubomi bonke.
- **Urhulumente:** jonga kubaphathiswa, amaziko oluntu, amaziko, kunye neenkonzo zabemi.
- **Ishishini:** izixhobo zasimahla kubemi ukuba baqale, basebenze, kwaye bakhulise ishishini.
- **IBRASA:** isango lokungena likawonke-wonke elaziwayo ilizwe elidibanisa inkqubo epheleleyo.

## Yakhelwe ilizwe ngokwelizwe

I-BRASA igcina iphepha lelizwe kwisizwe ngasinye esixhaswayo. Iphepha ngalinye lisebenzisa uyilo ekwabelwana ngalo kwihlabathi liphela ngelixa linxibelelanisa amaziko karhulumente welo lizwe, izixhobo zemfundo, kunye neenkonzo.

## Ukufikelela kulwimi

IsiNgesi lulwimi lwentsusa olugciniweyo lweBRASA. Iindwendwe zinokusebenzisa inguqulelo yasekhaya ngelixa abahleli beqhubeka besebenza ukusuka kumgangatho ofanayo wesiNgesi. Iinguqulelo ezipapashiweyo zifumana i-URL yazo, imetadata yolwimi, isimo sophononongo, kunye nekhonkco elibuyela kwisiNgesi.

## Qala apha

Ndwendwela [brasa.world](https://brasa.world/) kwaye ukhethe ilizwe lakho, ulwimi, okanye intsika yakho.

## Yiba negalelo

Nceda uqinisekise ilizwe, uphonononge inguqulelo, uxele ikhonkco loluntu eliphelelwe lixesha, okanye uphucule ukufikeleleka. Ukuchaneka kunye nolwazi oluluncedo lwasekuhlaleni luza ngaphambi komthamo wokuguqulela.

## BRASA namhlanje

BRASA ngoku idibanisa iindawo zamazwe ali-198 neMfundo, uRhulumente kunye noShishino. BRASA Business ineendawo ezili-12 ezidityanisiweyo: intengiso neerisithi, CRM, isitokhwe, abantu noqeqesho, umvuzo neebhanki, ixesha, inkxaso yosapho, umsebenzi wezomthetho, iifomu zikarhulumente, iingxelo nendawo yokusebenza ekwabelwana ngayo. Ikwabandakanya ileja evulekileyo, iinkqubo ezikhokelwayo kunye nokuqhubeka ngaphandle kwe-intanethi.

BRASA Financials idwelisa iinkqubo zentlawulo ezingama-385 ezivela kwiirekhodi ezingama-395, kuquka iinkcazo zemarike ezili-161 kunye neentlobo zentlawulo ezingama-216: imali yeselula, ukudluliselwa kwangoko, QR, izipaji zedijithali, uthungelwano lwemali, ukuthumela imali, open banking, amakhadi asekhaya kunye ne-USSD.

Ukudweliswa akuthethi ukuba sele kuvuliwe. Ababoneleli abanelayisensi bahambisa imali; indlela nganye ifuna ukuphononongwa kwelizwe nomthombo, ilayisensi, ukudityaniswa, uvavanyo lokuhlawula kunye nemvume yokuvelisa.
